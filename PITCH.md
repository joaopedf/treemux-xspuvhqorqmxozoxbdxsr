# MedBrief AI — Clinical Reasoning Copilot

**Every year, diagnostic errors contribute to 40,000-80,000 deaths in the US alone.** MedBrief AI is an AI-powered clinical reasoning copilot that transforms unstructured patient presentations into structured differential diagnoses, transparent reasoning pathways, and evidence-based workup recommendations — all streamed in real-time. Unlike existing clinical decision support tools that are rigid lookup databases, MedBrief AI reasons through cases the way an attending physician would: systematically extracting clinical data, weighing differentials with confidence scores, flagging red flags, and recommending prioritized workups (STAT vs routine). The interactive follow-up chat lets clinicians drill deeper into the analysis, ask "what if" questions, and explore alternative diagnoses. Built with GPT-4o streaming, Next.js, and a dark clinical-grade UI designed for high-stakes medical environments, MedBrief AI reimagines clinical decision support for the AI-native era — turning every physician into a physician with a world-class consult at their fingertips.
